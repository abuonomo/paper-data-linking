# Scientific Observation Instrumentation Form

## Summary of the Paper
- **Content Summary**: This paper analyzes a Parker Solar Probe first-orbit stream interaction region event near 0.33 au, focusing on energetic particle enhancements observed after a stream-interface passage in November 2018. The results are based on direct measurements from IS⊙IS energetic-particle data, specifically EPI-Hi and EPI-Lo proton/ion fluxes, together with SWEAP plasma measurements and FIELDS magnetic-field observations used to identify the stream interface and characterize the solar-wind context. The key observation interval is the post-interface energetic-particle enhancement on 2018-11-16 to 2018-11-22 (DOY 320–326), following a stream-interface passage near the end of 2018-11-14 (DOY 318). These data underpin the paper’s conclusions about sub-Parker spiral magnetic connectivity and suprathermal/energetic particle acceleration in stream interaction regions.

## Instrumentation Details

### EPI-Hi on board Parker Solar Probe
- **General Comments**:
  - Used for higher-energy energetic-particle measurements in the SIR event.
  - The analysis used LET1 A and B sides to compare sunward and anti-sunward proton fluxes.
  - Data were hourly binned and smoothed over 5 h intervals.
- **Supporting Quote**: "The EPI-Hi ﬂuxes are from the A and B sides of LET1, which are sunward and anti-sunward facing, respectively (A_ and B_H_Flux from psp_isois-epihi_l2-let1-rates3600)."

#### Data Collection Period 1: Post-stream-interface energetic particle enhancement
- **Time Range**: 2018-11-16 – 2018-11-22 (DOY 320–326), energetic particle enhancement after the stream interface passage during PSP’s first orbit
  - **Supporting Quote**: "Energetic particle enhancements were observed on the 320th–326th day of the year 2018, which corresponds to ∼1–7 days after the passage of the stream interface between faster and slower solar wind."
- **Wavelength(s)**: Not applicable; proton energy range 1.00-5.66 MeV
  - **Supporting Quote**: "The ﬂuxes are summed over energy bins spanning 1.00-5.66 MeV."
- **Physical Observable**: Proton fluxes
  - **Supporting Quote**: "The EPI-Hi data is comprised of all protons, and the EPI-Lo ﬂuxes are proton-dominated, but also include small contributions from other species."
- **Additional Comments**: The EPI-Hi measurements were used to examine directional fluxes and weak anisotropies associated with a distant SIR source.

### EPI-Lo on board Parker Solar Probe
- **General Comments**:
  - Used for lower-energy energetic-particle measurements during the same SIR event.
  - The analysis used Ion TOF-only proton-channel data from anti-sunward-facing wedges W0, W6, and W7.
  - Data were hourly binned and smoothed over 11 h intervals.
- **Supporting Quote**: "The EPI-Lo ﬂuxes are Ion TOF-only (H_Flux_ChanT from psp_isois-epilo_l2-ic)."

#### Data Collection Period 1: Post-stream-interface energetic particle enhancement
- **Time Range**: 2018-11-16 – 2018-11-22 (DOY 320–326), energetic particle enhancement after the stream interface passage during PSP’s first orbit
  - **Supporting Quote**: "Energetic particle enhancements were observed on the 320th–326th day of the year 2018, which corresponds to ∼1–7 days after the passage of the stream interface between faster and slower solar wind."
- **Wavelength(s)**: Not applicable; ion/proton energy range 166.47–496.27 keV
  - **Supporting Quote**: "They are averaged over the three anti-sunward-facing wedges (W0, W6 and W7; apertures,0-9 and 60-79) and summed over energy bins spanning 166.47–496.27 keV."
- **Physical Observable**: Proton-dominated ion fluxes
  - **Supporting Quote**: "The EPI-Hi data is comprised of all protons, and the EPI-Lo ﬂuxes are proton-dominated, but also include small contributions from other species."
- **Additional Comments**: These data were used for energy-time behavior of the lower-energy particle population and for comparison with higher-energy EPI-Hi fluxes.

### SWEAP on board Parker Solar Probe
- **General Comments**:
  - Used to identify the stream interface and provide the in situ plasma context for the SIR event.
  - The paper used SWEAP plasma observations for density and solar-wind-speed context and for deriving slower, faster, and intermediate stream speeds.
- **Supporting Quote**: "The stream interfaces were identiﬁed using in situ plasma observations from the Solar Wind Electrons Alphas and Protons Investigation (SWEAP; Kasper et al. 2016) ..."

#### Data Collection Period 1: Stream-interface passage and solar-wind context for the SIR event
- **Time Range**: 2018-11-14 (late DOY 318), stream-interface passage preceding the post-interface energetic-particle enhancement
  - **Supporting Quote**: "The passage of the stream interface occurs near the end of day 318."
- **Wavelength(s)**: Not applicable for in situ plasma measurements
  - **Supporting Quote**: "The stream interfaces were identiﬁed using in situ plasma observations from the Solar Wind Electrons Alphas and Protons Investigation (SWEAP; Kasper et al. 2016) ..."
- **Physical Observable**: Solar-wind plasma observations, including stream speeds within and around the SIR
  - **Supporting Quote**: "We observe the faster stream with radial speed Vh ∼530 km s−1, whereas the slower stream moves with radial speed Vl ∼320 km s−1, and the intermediate speed wind within the SIR has radial speed Vm ∼425 km s−1."
- **Additional Comments**: SWEAP measurements were central to locating the stream interface and defining the plasma structure used in the SIR interpretation.

### FIELDS on board Parker Solar Probe
- **General Comments**:
  - Used with SWEAP to identify the stream interface and provide in situ magnetic-field context for the event.
  - The paper also used FIELDS magnetic-field measurements in the event overview.
- **Supporting Quote**: "The stream interfaces were identiﬁed using in situ plasma observations from the Solar Wind Electrons Alphas and Protons Investigation (SWEAP; Kasper et al. 2016) and the in situ magnetic ﬁeld observations from the electromagnetic ﬁelds investigation, commonly referred to as FIELDS (Bale et al. 2016)."

#### Data Collection Period 1: Stream-interface passage and magnetic-field context for the SIR event
- **Time Range**: 2018-11-14 (late DOY 318), stream-interface passage preceding the post-interface energetic-particle enhancement
  - **Supporting Quote**: "The passage of the stream interface occurs near the end of day 318."
- **Wavelength(s)**: Not applicable for in situ magnetic-field measurements
  - **Supporting Quote**: "the in situ magnetic ﬁeld observations from the electromagnetic ﬁelds investigation, commonly referred to as FIELDS"
- **Physical Observable**: In situ magnetic field observations
  - **Supporting Quote**: "magnetic ﬁeld measured by FIELDS."
- **Additional Comments**: FIELDS data provided the magnetic context used together with SWEAP to identify the stream interface associated with the analyzed SIR event.
