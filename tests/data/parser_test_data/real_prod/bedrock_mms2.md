# Scientific Observation Instrumentation Form

## Summary of the Paper
- **Content Summary**: This study investigates Earth’s magnetosphere response to a sub‑Alfvénic solar wind driven by a coronal mass ejection (CME) on 24 April 2023. Using high‑cadence, three‑dimensional plasma and field measurements from the Magnetospheric Multiscale (MMS) mission, supplemented by upstream data from the Wind spacecraft and THEMIS B, the authors document the disappearance of the dayside bow shock, the emergence of Alfvén‑wing filaments that magnetically connect Earth to the Sun’s erupted flux rope, and the capture of cold CME plasma on closed field lines. The analysis focuses on a two‑hour sub‑Alfvénic interval (~12:30 UT–14:30 UT) during which the Alfvén Mach number stays ≈0.6, and on associated energetic particle signatures (60–560 keV electrons/ions) that reveal reconnection processes. These observations provide direct evidence of Alfvén‑wing formation and plasma exchange in a planetary magnetosphere under sub‑Alfvénic conditions.
