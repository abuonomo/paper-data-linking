# Scientific Observation Instrumentation Form

## Summary of the Paper
- **Content Summary**: This study analyzes energetic particle enhancements associated with stream interaction regions (SIRs) observed by Parker Solar Probe (PSP) during its first orbit. Using in‑situ measurements from the Integrated Science Investigation of the Sun (IS⊙IS) energetic‑particle suite (EPI‑Hi and EPI‑Lo), plasma parameters from the Solar Wind Electrons Alphas and Protons Investigation (SWEAP), and magnetic‑field data from FIELDS, the authors examine particle fluxes over the 320th–326th day of 2018 (≈2018‑11‑16 to 2018‑11‑22) when a stream interface passed the spacecraft at ~0.33 au. The six‑day observation interval provides a new view of particle acceleration and transport in the inner heliosphere, highlighting the role of sub‑Parker spiral magnetic connectivity.

## Instrumentation Details

### Integrated Science Investigation of the Sun (IS⊙IS) on board Parker Solar Probe
- **General Comments**:
  - The IS⊙IS suite supplies energetic‑particle measurements from two instruments, EPI‑Hi (higher‑energy) and EPI‑Lo (lower‑energy), covering ~0.02–200 MeV nucleon⁻¹.
  - Separate sunward and anti‑sunward telescopes allow assessment of particle anisotropy.
- **Supporting Quote**: ... Integrated Science Investigation of the Sun (IS⊙IS) instrument suite (McComas et al. 2016), which is comprised of two Energetic Particle Instruments measuring Higher (EPI‑Hi) and Lower (EPI‑Lo) energy particles (McComas et al. 2016) over the range 0.02–200 MeV/nucleon.

#### Data Collection Period 1: Energetic particle fluxes from the SIR event
- **Time Range**: 2018-11-16 to 2018-11-22 (DOY 320–326)
  - **Supporting Quote**: Energetic particle enhancements were observed on the 320th–326th day of the year 2018, which corresponds to ∼1–7 days after the passage of the stream interface between faster and slower solar wind.
- **Wavelength(s)**: 1.00–5.66 MeV (EPI‑Hi); 166.47–496.27 keV (EPI‑Lo)
  - **Supporting Quote**: The fluxes are summed over energy bins spanning 1.00-5.66 MeV.  
  - **Supporting Quote**: They are averaged over the three anti‑sunward facing telescopes and summed over energy bins spanning 166.47–496.27 keV.
- **Physical Observable**: Proton fluxes (sunward and anti‑sunward directions)
  - **Supporting Quote**: The EPI‑Hi fluxes are from the A and B sides of LET1, which are sunward and anti‑sunward facing, respectively (A_ and B_H_Flux from psp_isois-epihi_l2-let1‑rates3600).
- **Additional Comments**:
  - The EPI‑Hi data are binned hourly and box‑car averaged over 5 h intervals.  
    - **Supporting Quote**: The data are binned hourly and then smoothed (boxcar averaged) over 5 h intervals.  
  - The EPI‑Lo data are binned hourly and smoothed over 11 h intervals.  
    - **Supporting Quote**: The data are binned hourly and smoothed over 11 h intervals.
