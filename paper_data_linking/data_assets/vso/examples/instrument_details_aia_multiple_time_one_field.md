# Scientific Observation Instrumentation Form

## Summary of the Paper
- **Content Summary**: This paper reports on Sun-as-a-star analyses of spatially integrated Hα spectra to study a variety of solar active events – including flares, filament eruptions, and off‐limb prominence eruptions. The main observational dataset is provided by the Solar Dynamics Doppler Imager (SDDI) mounted on the Solar Magnetic Activity Research Telescope (SMART) at Hida Observatory, Kyoto University. Complementary data from GOES soft X-ray measurements, LASCO coronagraph images onboard SOHO, and multi-wavelength imagery from the Atmospheric Imaging Assembly (AIA) on board the Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO) are used to (i) identify event timing, (ii) verify CME occurrence, and (iii) ensure accurate event scenario interpretation. The event dates span from November 2016 to May 2021, with the routine full-disk Hα observations by SMART/SDDI beginning in May 2016.

## Instrumentation Details

### 1. SMART/SDDI on board the Solar Magnetic Activity Research Telescope (SMART)
- **General Comments**:
   - This instrument is the backbone of the study. It captures full-disk solar images in the Hα wavelengths and is used to perform Sun-as-a-star analyses by integrating spectral images over selected target regions. It has been operating routinely since May 2016, covering all events analyzed (from 2016 November up to 2021 May).
   - **Supporting Quote**: "The Solar Dynamics Doppler Imager (SDDI; Ichimoto et al. 2017) is installed on the Solar Magnetic Activity Research Telescope (SMART; Ueno et al. 2004) at Hida Observatory, Kyoto University. It takes full-disk solar images at 73 wavelengths from the Hα line center −9.0 ˚A to the Hα line center +9.0 ˚A with a time cadence of 12−16 sec... The SDDI has been observing the Sun routinely since 2016 May and has stored the Hα spectral images of various active events."

#### Data Collection Periods

##### Data Collection Period (General Operational Period)
- **Time Range**: Since May 2016 through at least May 2021
   - **Supporting Quote**: "...has been observing the Sun routinely since 2016 May..."
- **Wavelength(s)**: 73 wavelengths spanning from Hα line center −9.0 ˚A to +9.0 ˚A
   - **Supporting Quote**: "...at 73 wavelengths from the Hα line center −9.0 ˚A to the Hα line center +9.0 ˚A..."
- **Physical Observable**: Hα spectral profiles showing emission, absorption, red/blue shifts, and line broadening which are linked to flares, filament eruptions, and prominence motions.
   - **Supporting Quote**: "...Sun-as-a-star Hα spectra exhibit different features depending on the causes of the emission: (i) Flares show emission at the Hα line center with red asymmetry and line broadening; (ii) Filament eruptions show blue-/red-shifted absorptions; (iii) Eruptions of offlimb prominences show shifted emissions."
- **Additional Comments**: Spatial integration methods and subsequent calibration enable comparison with stellar observations.
